**Figure 1. Study flow and cohort composition.**
Five publicly available collections from The Cancer Imaging Archive (TCIA) contributed 781 imaging entries, corresponding to 741 unique patients after de-duplication by original patient identifier. NSCLC-Radiomics (LUNG1, n = 422) and NSCLC-Radiogenomics (n = 211) formed the 633-patient survival cohort, with LUNG1 used for model development and NSCLC-Radiogenomics used for external validation. LungCT-Diagnosis (n = 61), QIN-LUNG-CT (n = 47) and LUAD-CT-Survival (n = 40) were not used for survival model development; LUAD-CT-Survival (20 long, 20 short) was used only as a diagnostic binary-endpoint evaluation. Twenty-four LungCT-Diagnosis cases share a 30-digit StudyInstanceUID suffix with corresponding LUAD-CT-Survival/QIN-LUNG-CT cases, which would reduce the unique patient count to 717 if these were treated as shared patients. LUNG1 and NSCLC-Radiogenomics share no patients by either patient identifier or StudyInstanceUID. Blue boxes indicate the three core counts reported in the Results. Panel letter a is shown in bold lowercase. TCIA, The Cancer Imaging Archive.
