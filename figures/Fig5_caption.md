**Figure 5. Cross-cohort agreement of fitted Cox coefficients.**
(a) Coefficients of the 89 components of the joint model fitted separately in LUNG1 (n = 422) and NSCLC-Radiogenomics (n = 211); each point is one component. Orange squares (s = 28) indicate the 19 components forming the union of the ten components with the largest absolute coefficient in each cohort; grey circles (s = 12) indicate the remaining components; the black dashed line is the identity line and the thin grey lines mark zero. Marker shape, not colour alone, distinguishes the two classes, so the panel remains interpretable in grayscale. (b) The ten components with the largest absolute coefficient in LUNG1, shown as paired bars giving the coefficient fitted in LUNG1 (blue, solid) and the corresponding coefficient fitted in NSCLC-Radiogenomics (green, hatched); hatching is a redundant cue that survives grayscale reproduction. The inset box in (a) reports the Spearman correlation between the two coefficient vectors, ρ = 0.223 (p = 0.036), and the overlap of the top-10 components, 10% (1 of 10). Coefficients were fitted with the same model specification in both cohorts, so differences reflect the fitted coefficients rather than differences in model construction.
