**Supplementary Figure S6. Descriptive overall survival by cohort.** Kaplan-Meier estimates for the two survival-analysis cohorts, LUNG1 (n = 422) and NSCLC-Radiogenomics (n = 211), from the available survival times and event indicators. Shaded bands show the confidence limits returned by the `survival::survfit` calculation. Curves describe cohort composition and are not model-based risk strata or a test of model discrimination. Time is measured in years from baseline. The figure is generated from the unchanged clinical source data by `make_figures_final.R`.
